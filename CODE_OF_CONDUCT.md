# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, give actionable
technical feedback, assume good faith, and make the project welcoming to people
of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, publishing
another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other project
spaces.

## Technical discussion

Disagreement about engineering choices is welcome. Focus criticism on the code,
evidence, safety boundary, and documented requirements. Do not turn technical
disagreement into comments about a contributor's character or ability.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these
standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. When
possible, enforcement decisions will explain the behavior that must change.

Use GitHub's content-reporting controls for abusive content. Do not use a public
issue to disclose private information or report a security vulnerability;
security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies whenever someone represents the project or participates in
project-managed spaces.
